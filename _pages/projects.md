## Beacon: Blackout Driving Dataset and Traffic Control
*University of Tennessee, Knoxville · August 2023 – December 2024*

Built **Beacon**, a real-world traffic dataset recorded during blackouts, and applied Rainbow DQN to control robot vehicles in the reconstructed traffic, reducing wait times by up to 82.6%.

## Driving Maneuver Classification from Time-Series Data
*Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology · March 2017 – February 2022*

Developed domain-knowledge-based machine learning methods for classifying driving maneuvers from time-series sensor data, including an LSTM classifier for seven maneuvers with over 98% per-class accuracy, transfer learning, and an interpretable rule-based approach. This work formed my M.S. thesis.

- **Role:** Graduate Research Assistant
- **Outcomes:** 4 publications; Best Paper Award (Honorable Mention), ICAICT 2020

[[Code]](https://github.com/Supriya-Sarker/driving-maneuvers-classification-supervised-approach-)

**Related publications**

- [Driving maneuver classification using domain-specific knowledge and transfer learning](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/9455147), *IEEE Access*, 2021
- [A data change rule-based empirical framework for labeling unlabeled time series driving data](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/9333495), *ICAICT*, 2020

## Hybrid Digital Watermarking for Copyright Protection
*Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology · March 2014 – December 2014*

Developed a hybrid digital watermarking algorithm based on the Discrete Wavelet Transform to improve copyright protection, with a focus on robustness to noise and compression while preserving image quality.

- **Role:** Graduate Research Assistant
- **Outcomes:** Journal paper, *Advancement in Image Processing and Pattern Recognition*, 2018

[[Paper]](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/331384894_Hybrid_Digital_Watermarking_Scheme_Based_on_Discrete_Wavelet_Transform_for_Copyright_Protection)
