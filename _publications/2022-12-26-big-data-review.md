### Overview
This paper reviews big data technologies used in power, mineral, and manufacturing industries, focusing on how large-scale industrial data is leveraged to improve efficiency, automation, and decision-making.

### Contributions
- Systematic review and metadata-driven analysis of industrial big data literature  
- Taxonomy of big data application areas across major industrial sectors  
- Identification of key research gaps and future research directions for big data-driven industries
